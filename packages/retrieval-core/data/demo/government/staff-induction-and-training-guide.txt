Staff Induction and Training Guide
Fictional Department of Digital Services (FDDS)
Synthetic demonstration document — not a real government record.

1. Purpose
This guide describes the induction and onboarding process for new starters at FDDS, including ongoing mandatory training obligations for Australian Public Service (APS) employees.

2. Before your first day
People Services will provision your official device, email account and building access pass. You will be assigned a workplace buddy and a supervisor who will complete a first-day checklist with you, covering security awareness, code of conduct obligations, and where to find departmental policies and procedures.

3. First week — induction program
New starters complete the Commonwealth-wide APS induction modules covering the APS Values, the APS Code of Conduct, work health and safety, and appropriate use of official information. Department-specific induction covers information security (see the Information Security Policy), records management, privacy obligations, and how to raise a complaint or grievance.

4. Mandatory annual training
All staff must complete annual refresher training in information security awareness, fraud and corruption awareness, and workplace health and safety. Staff with financial delegations must additionally complete procurement and PGPA Act training before exercising their delegation. Completion is tracked by People Services and reported to the FDDS Governance Committee.

5. Ongoing support
Staff can access guidance, policy documents and support contacts through the department's intranet at any time. Questions about onboarding, entitlements or training requirements should be directed to People Services.
